When employees work on planned shifts, their leave durations must be
computed over the shift actually assigned instead of the regular working
calendar:

- A full day leave covers the whole shift starting on that day,
  including night shifts that end past midnight.
- A half day leave covers half of the shift hours.
- Half day and hour based leaves don't flag the shift line as on leave,
  as the employee is still expected to work part of the shift.

Usage
=====

No configuration is needed. Once installed, leave durations of employees
with assigned shifts are computed over the shift instead of the regular
working calendar:

- Request a full day leave on a day with an assigned shift: the duration
  is the whole shift, even for night shifts ending past midnight.
- Request a half day leave: the duration is half of the shift hours and
  the shift line remains assigned, as the employee still works part of
  it.
