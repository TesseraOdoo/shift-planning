When employees work on planned shifts, their leave durations must be computed
over the shift actually assigned instead of the regular working calendar:

- A full day leave covers the whole shift starting on that day, including night
  shifts that end past midnight.
- A half day leave covers half of the shift hours.
- Half day and hour based leaves don't flag the shift line as on leave, as the
  employee is still expected to work part of the shift.
